How many words — ranking gap

Which words — repeat score R

Threshold fitted on the 10 clean models only. With 10 clips per speaker, R > 0.10 means the same 3-word phrase was inserted in at least two of one speaker's clips. Clean models never did; every backdoored model did in 3–5. Repaired models are held out and fall back to the clean level.
Evaluation · blinded re-run

Names hidden. Threshold frozen. Same answers.

Every model, speaker and clip was replaced by a random code. The scorer never saw a label, a target or the payload. Verdicts were hashed before the key was opened.

Repair · base paper GN-FT (IEEE ICASSP 2025)

Repair, then audit again

The auditor agrees with the repairAll six repaired models drop to R = 0.10, the clean level. Re-audited, each one is cleared.
The control mattersPlain fine-tuning on 25 clean clips removes our backdoor as completely as GN-FT, in half the time. That says our attack is too weak to compare repair methods, not that GN-FT fails.
So we focus on detectionRepair stays as the base-paper baseline. The auditor is the contribution.
